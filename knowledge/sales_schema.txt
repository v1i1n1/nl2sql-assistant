TABLE: sales

DESCRIPTION:
Contains customer sales transactions.

COLUMNS:

sale_id:
Unique identifier for each sales transaction.

customer_id:
Identifier of the customer associated with the transaction.
Foreign key referencing customers.customer_id.

product_id:
Identifier of the product associated with the transaction.
Foreign key referencing products.product_id.

amount:
Transaction amount.

sale_date:
Date on which the sales transaction occurred.

status:
Status of the transaction.
Expected values include completed and cancelled.

RELATIONSHIPS:

sales.customer_id -> customers.customer_id

sales.product_id -> products.product_id

BUSINESS RULE:
Revenue and sales calculations should use the amount column
from completed transactions.

Cancelled transactions should not be included in revenue
calculations.
