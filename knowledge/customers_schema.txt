TABLE: customers

DESCRIPTION:
Contains customer information.

COLUMNS:

customer_id:
Unique identifier for each customer.

customer_name:
Name of the customer.

email:
Email address associated with the customer.

region_id:
Identifier of the customer's region.
Foreign key referencing regions.region_id.

customer_type:
Business classification of the customer.

created_at:
Timestamp indicating when the customer record was created.

RELATIONSHIP:

customers.region_id -> regions.region_id
